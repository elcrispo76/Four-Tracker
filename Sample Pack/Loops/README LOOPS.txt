FOUR TRACKER LOOPS  v1.0
========================

44 loops (WAV, 44.1 kHz / 16-bit stereo), made from the sounds in this sample pack, so
they are CC0 like the rest of the pack: use them in any music, commercial or not, and
share them, no credit needed.

  Drums/DnB          174 BPM: two step, roller, jungle break, half time, liquid, chops
  Drums/Breakbeats   136 and 160 BPM breaks
  Drums/Techno       130 BPM
  Drums/House        124 BPM (a little swing)
  Drums/Top Loops    hats, shaker, percussion and ride loops without kick
  Bass/DnB           174 BPM, F minor (reese, sub, rolling)
  Bass/Techno        130 BPM, A minor (offbeat, rolling, acid, squelch)
  Bass/House         124 BPM, G minor (offbeat, deep, octave)
  Arps               trance, pulse, house keys, DnB bells, supersaw
  Chords             house stabs, dub techno chords, DnB plucks

THE FILE NAME SAYS IT ALL
  "HOUSE BEAT 01 CLASSIC 124 BPM 16ST" is 1 bar (16 steps) at 124 BPM, cut exactly, so it
  loops without a seam. 32ST = 2 bars. Bass, arps and chords also give the key.

IN FOUR TRACKER
  * Set the song to the loop's BPM (SPEED 6) and play the loop on C-4: 16 steps = 16 rows.
  * The WAV loop points are set over the whole file, so LOOP is on when you load them.
  * Cut a beat up with 09xx: SAMPLE EDIT > OFFSET, choose 16 (or 32) steps, and you get
    the code for every step. 09FF reaches 65280 frames (1.48 s): at 174 BPM a whole bar
    fits; at 124 - 136 BPM the last steps of the bar can't be reached with 09xx
    (the calculator shows "--" there).
  * Loops that go together have the same tempo and key: e.g. the 124 BPM house beat,
    house bass, keys and stabs in G minor.

make_loops.py is the script that built them from the pack's one-shots.
