FOUR TRACKER SAMPLE PACK  v1.1
==============================

88 WAV samples and 44 loops (44.1 kHz / 16-bit) for Four Tracker - or any tracker,
sampler or DAW.
Every sound was synthesised from scratch for this pack (drums with
code, bass and synth sounds with Four Tracker's own SYNTH 2), so no
third-party recordings are included.

CONTENTS
  Drums/Kicks, Snares, Claps, Hats, Cymbals, Toms, Percussion
  FX/        risers, downlifter, impact, laser, noise hit
  Bass/      acid, squelch, rolling, octave, 80s, sub, reese, pluck, square
  Synth/Leads    supersaw, hoover, arp, sync, square, dream
  Synth/Plucks   trance pluck, poly stab, space bell, soft keys, pulse, 80s pluck
  Synth/Brass    italo brass, jump brass
  Synth/Pads     juno, strings, choir, 80s strings, gated, sweep
  Synth/Chords   one-shot chords (minor, major, min7, sus4, maj7) on root C
  Synth/Chip     tiny looped chip waveforms (64 frames, loop points set)
  Loops/     44 tempo-exact loops: DnB, breakbeats, techno and house beats,
             top loops, basslines, arps and chords (see Loops/README LOOPS.txt)

TIPS FOR FOUR TRACKER
  * The note in the file name is the root note (C2, C3, C4, C5). Load
    a sample and play it on that note to hear it at its original pitch.
  * The CHIP waves carry WAV loop points, so Four Tracker (and most
    samplers) loops them automatically - play them like a chip synth.
  * Bass sounds are mono; synth sounds are stereo (chorus/delay/reverb).
  * Peaks are normalised to -1 dB. Turn the instrument volume down
    when you stack chords or pads.
